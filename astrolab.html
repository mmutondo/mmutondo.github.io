Welcome to Mutondo Astrobiology Lab

Exploring life beyond Earth, starting from right here in Zambia.

This is What We Do

We investigate the possibilities of extraterrestrial life by combining biology, chemistry, and planetary science. Our research focuses on extreme environments and simulation experiments that mimic conditions found on Mars, Europa, and other celestial bodies.
