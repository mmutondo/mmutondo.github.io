Tools

Explore our advanced tools and methodologies that drive our astrobiology research.

Microscopy

Our lab utilizes cutting-edge microscopy to visualize microorganisms in extreme environments, providing detailed insights into their cellular structures and functions.

Spectrometry

We employ advanced spectrometers to analyze the chemical composition of extraterrestrial samples, identifying key biosignatures and organic molecules.

Modeling

Our team develops sophisticated computational models to simulate the conditions on distant planets, predicting the potential for habitability and life.

Fieldwork

We conduct field research in extreme environments on Earth, such as volcanic vents and polar regions, to study analog sites for extraterrestrial life.

Focus

Our research focuses on understanding the origins and distribution of life in the universe. We use a variety of techniques.

Meteorites

We analyze the molecular composition of meteorites.
