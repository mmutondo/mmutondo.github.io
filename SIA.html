Why work with me

I built an international science career from Zambia. I did my postgraduate studies in Europe, where I earned a place in the International Max Planck Research School at the Max Planck Institute for Plant Breeding Research in Cologne, Germany. My love for extremophiles guided my postdoctoral research into life in extreme environments such as Antarctica. Today I'm a university lecturer teaching biotechnology, and I'm a National Geographic Explorer working with their SPACE portfolio.

That is my science story. What is yours going to be?

For years, students and colleagues have come to me for advice on applications, careers and the hard parts of academic life. Sunbird Innovation Agency is how I make that help available to more people.
