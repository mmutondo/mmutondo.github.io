Welcome to Unicorn Brainery

Unicorn Brainery is your trusted partner in building a thriving bioscience career in Africa. We empower students, young professionals, and organizations with the expertise, mentorship, and resources needed to unlock world-class opportunities in the biosciences.

What We Do

Personalized Career Consulting

  • Tailored guidance for students and graduates seeking direction in biotechnology, biomedicine, pharma, and related fields.
  • Support with resume building, interview preparation, and research proposal development.

Study & Scholarship Navigation

  • Assistance in identifying local and international study programs and scholarship opportunities.
  • Step-by-step advice for successful scholarship applications and admissions.

Capacity Building

  • Workshops, webinars, and upskilling sessions led by bioscience experts.
  • Mentorship programs with accomplished African professionals in academia, industry, and entrepreneurship.

Industry Networking

  • Connecting you with African and global bioscience leaders through events, seminars, and strategic introductions.
  • Assistance with internship, research, and job placements.
