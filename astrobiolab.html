About

At Mutondo Astrobiology Laboratory, we investigate the nature and origins of life in the universe using interdisciplinary approaches including biology, chemistry, geology and planetary science.

Research

  • Origin of Life
  • Martian Analogues
  • Characterisation of natural African extreme environments
  • Anthropogenic environments as analogues for extraterrestrial habitats
  • Biotechnology of extremophiles
