Student Research Projects

PhD, MSc and BSc projects from the Mutondo Astrobiology Laboratory, grouped by theme.

← Back to the lab

Life in extreme and human-made environments

Mining sites and polluted waters as stand-ins for harsh environments on other worlds.

  • PhD Potentially harmful elements in Nkana Black Mountain slag and exploitation of associated microbiota
  • BSc Heavy-metal remediation by cyanobacteria from industrial wastewater
  • BSc Mycoremediation of wastewater from Hippo Dam, Chingola, using Aspergillus niger
  • BSc Antibiotic-resistant algae in natural and anthropogenic environments of Kitwe
  • BSc Gut microbes of wax worms in plastic biodegradation

Cyanobacteria: from Zambian waters to life support

The organisms that first oxygenated Earth, studied as partners for growing food in hard places.

  • BSc Morphotyping cyanobacteria in Zambian waterways (Kafue River)
  • BSc Development and optimisation of cyanobiochar biofertiliser
  • BSc Biochar and microalgae as a combined biofertiliser for crop growth

Life's chemical toolkit: cyclotides and bioactive compounds

Unusually tough plant molecules that hint at how life builds stable chemistry.

  • MSc Identification and characterisation of bioactive compounds in Cassia abbreviata
  • MSc Cyclotides from Zambian Cucurbitaceae and Poaceae for potential insecticidal applications
  • BSc Cyclotides from local plants as potential acaricides against ticks
  • BSc Haemolytic activity of cyclotides from Solanum torvum, S. aethiopicum and Vigna unguiculata

Reading and designing life's molecules with computers

Bioinformatics and deep learning to find and design proteins.

  • BSc Discovery of cyclotide precursors in the Solanum aethiopicum genome
  • BSc Characterisation and validation of cyclotide domains in Cassia abbreviata
  • BSc Inverse protein folding of cyclotides in Solanum aethiopicum using deep learning
  • BSc Inverse protein folding of the Arp2/3 complex in Solanum aethiopicum using deep learning

Other biotech projects

  • BSc Detecting meat adulteration using DNA technology and bioinformatics
  • BSc Antifungal and antibacterial properties of soy yoghurt infused with Bidens pilosa
  • BSc Oat yoghurt infused with functional foods
  • BSc Protein biomarkers for psychiatric disorders (depression, schizophrenia and bipolar disorder)
